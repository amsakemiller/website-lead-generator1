# Lead Generation Setup

---

## [ Answer One Prompt Instead ]

Describe your business and ideal customers in your own words. Include: what you sell, who buys it (industry, size, geography), who doesn't, example customers, and decision-maker titles.

**Example of a great answer:**

> **BoxFlow** (boxflow.io) sells warehouse management software to mid-size e-commerce companies in the US. Deals range $30K-$150K/year.
>
> **Ideal customer:** $5M-$100M revenue, 50-500 employees, ships 1000+ orders/day, currently using spreadsheets or outdated WMS. Recently raised funding or opened new warehouse = strong buying signal.
>
> **Sell to:** VP/Director of Operations, Logistics, or Supply Chain. Sometimes COO at smaller companies.
>
> **Don't sell to:** 3PLs (competitors' customers), companies already on SAP/Oracle, companies under $3M revenue, outside US.
>
> **Current customers:** acme-shipping.com, fastfulfill.io, boxbrand.com
>
> **Search terms by category:**
> - Industry: e-commerce, DTC brand, online retail, Shopify seller
> - Problem: fulfillment challenges, warehouse inefficiency, inventory management
> - Stage: Series A, growing, scaling, hiring warehouse staff
> - Tech: WMS, 3PL alternative, ShipStation, fulfillment software
